削除の依頼方法
  1. メール francepan10299@gmail.com に、件名「データ削除の依頼」で送ってください。
  2. プレミアム（定期購入）の方は、Google Play の注文番号（GPA.xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxx）を本文に書いてください。注文番号から、あなたの記録を探します。
  3. 30日以内に返信し、確認できた記録を60日以内に削除します。

English

How to request deletion
  1. Email francepan10299@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. If you have Premium (subscription), include your Google Play order number (GPA.xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxx) so we can find your records.
  3. We reply within 30 days and delete the records we can identify within 60 days.
DataWhat it isRetention / deletion
Chats and quiz results on your deviceStored only on your deviceDeleted at once via Settings → Delete all chats, or by uninstalling the app. We do not hold them.
Anonymous ID (UID)A random ID. We do not collect name, email or phone numberRemoved from your device on uninstall. Server-side records are deleted on request.
Usage countsChats per day, linked to the UID and dateKept while needed to run the service. Identified records are deleted on request.
Purchase informationPurchase token and Premium expiryKept while the subscription runs, to verify Premium. Deleted on request (Premium can no longer be verified afterwards).
Reply reportsThe reported reply text, reason, language, philosopher ID and time (not what you wrote)Kept up to 2 years for quality review. Identified records are deleted on request.
Ad-related dataHandled by Google AdMobManage it at adssettings.google.com.

Server records are tied only to a random ID and contain no name or similar data. Because you cannot see the ID yourself, non-Premium users should include their device model and the approximate period of use in the email. If we cannot identify records, we will tell you.
